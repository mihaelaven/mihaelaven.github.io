hello, world

      
I build products for a living and small experiments for fun. Curiosity is my fuel. I like taking an idea far enough to see how it works, where it falls short and how it can be improved. Technology keeps changing and building things helps me understand it and form my own perspective.

      
This portfolio is an evolving collection of experimentation, public artifacts I’ve built and a homage to things I enjoy.

# Mihaela

> Product leader with experience in B2B and consumer products. This site collects apps, AI experiments and reusable skills, plus books read and other interests. Open to fractional and contract product work.

## Pages
- Home (#home): who I am and what this site is
- Toolkit (#toolkit): open-source skills and small apps for product managers on GitHub. Each entry says what it is and how to install it
- Apps (#apps): Knijko, a live book-listing app on Google Play and the App Store (https://knijko.bg), and Three Eras
- Books (#books): books read and currently reading, synced from Goodreads
- AI x botany (#botany): a series of AI-generated botanical illustrations
- Contact (#contact): links to LinkedIn, GitHub and Goodreads

## Toolkit
Skills (install with python3 scripts/install-skills.py <name> from https://github.com/mihaelaven/product-toolkit):
- prd: turns an idea into a one-page brief or a full PRD
- feature-request-triage: groups requests into underlying needs and recommends what to build next
- client-validation-analysis: checks interview notes against a hypothesis
- pm-vault: keeps a decision log
- linear-task-creation: drafts Linear issues from notes or a transcript
- content-guidelines: writes or reviews copy against a guideline

Workflows (n8n, in https://github.com/mihaelaven/product-toolkit/tree/main/workflows):
- shipped-digest: a weekly digest of finished Linear issues posted to Slack and Notion
- feedback-triage: sorts Slack feedback and proposes an action for a person to approve

Agents:
- scope-drift-checker: compares project scope with meeting decisions. https://github.com/mihaelaven/drift-tracker
- pm-news-digest: writes a verified daily product-management news digest. https://github.com/mihaelaven/pm-news-desk

Apps:
- capacity-timeline-planner, call-task-hub, drift-tracker, pm-news-desk: https://github.com/mihaelaven
